Existing patients

Getting help during treatment

True Care patients can reach the clinic by phone (Hamilton (289) 674-9676 or Mississauga (289) 674-9662), Monday–Friday, 7:00 AM–3:00 PM, for appointments, monitoring, results and medication questions. If you have severe or worrying symptoms, don’t wait for a call back — go to the nearest emergency department or call 911.

Call your clinicLocations & hours

Go to emergency or call 911 for severe abdominal pain, heavy bleeding (soaking a pad an hour), fainting, chest pain or difficulty breathing, or a fever after a procedure. After egg retrieval, also go if you have rapidly increasing bloating, sudden weight gain, or are passing very little urine.

During clinic hours

Call reception — Hamilton (289) 674-9676 or Mississauga (289) 674-9662 — for appointments, monitoring times, results and medication questions.

After hours

If you are in an active treatment cycle, follow the after-hours instructions your care team gave you. For anything urgent, go to the nearest emergency department.

Your health information

Please don’t send symptoms or results by email or the website form — they aren’t secure. Call the clinic, or bring documents to your appointment.
