When to seek help

When should you see a fertility specialist?

Infertility affects about 1 in 6 people worldwide at some point in their lives (World Health Organization, 2023). Consider asking your doctor for a referral if you have been trying to conceive for:

  • 12 months if you are under 35
  • 6 months if you are 35 or older

Ask sooner if your periods are irregular or absent, you have known endometriosis or PCOS, you’ve had two or more pregnancy losses, your partner has a known sperm issue, you plan to use donor eggs or sperm, or you are about to start treatment that could affect fertility.

Your plan

How treatment is planned

  1. Assess

    Testing for you and your partner — bloodwork, ultrasound and semen analysis, largely on site.

  2. Explain

    A results review in person or by video, in plain language, with every option laid out.

  3. Treat

    From monitored cycles and IUI to IVF, frozen transfer and genetic testing, as needed.

  4. Support

    Nursing, medication teaching, and genetic, nutritional and psychological counselling.

Questions

Fertility questions

What is the difference between IUI and IVF?

In IUI, prepared sperm is placed in the uterus and fertilization happens inside the body. In IVF, eggs are retrieved and fertilized in the laboratory, and an embryo is transferred back. IVF involves more steps and medication, and is used when IUI is unlikely to work or hasn’t worked.

Do you offer donor egg and donor sperm treatment?

Yes — donor sperm IUI, and IVF with donor eggs or donor sperm, all with anonymous donors only. Gestational carrier cycles are coordinated as well.

Do I need a referral for fertility care?

Yes. All new patients need a referral from an Ontario physician, faxed to (855) 694-9327. See new patients.

Can appointments be virtual?

Consultations and results reviews can often be done by video. Monitoring, bloodwork, ultrasound and procedures are done at the clinic.

Ready to take the next step?

New patients need a referral from an Ontario physician. Ask your doctor to fax our referral form to (855) 694-9327, then complete your intake form.
