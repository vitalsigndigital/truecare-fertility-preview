Medical Office Administrator (MOA)

Operations · Reports to Director of Operations and Nursing Services

The Medical Office Administrator (MOA) will be involved in all aspects of administering the daily operations of True Care Fertility, promoting the clinic's diverse services, and expanding its client base. As the front-face of our clinic, the MOA plays a critical role in supporting the team and maintaining a professional and welcoming environment.

Responsibilities

  • Consult with physicians as needed regarding scheduling and booking appointments
  • Actively participate in team-building initiatives and development of departmental goals
  • Work independently with minimal supervision
  • Maintain up-to-date patient records in the EMR system, including scanning and organizing test results and other documents
  • Handle administrative tasks such as sending/receiving faxes, making photocopies, scanning documents, and accepting deliveries
  • Administer patient billing, collect overdue payments, and issue receipts
  • Ensure confidentiality of all patient information in accordance with regulatory standards
  • Perform other duties as assigned

Education

  • Associate's Degree in Medical Office Administration, or a Bachelor's Degree in Healthcare Management or another relevant field
  • Additional vocational training or certification in a medical field is preferred

Experience

  • Minimum 3 years of experience as a Medical Office Administrator

Competencies

  • Proficient understanding of medical terminology, including common conditions, procedures, and abbreviations
  • Keen attention to detail when recording patient information, managing medical records, and processing billing
  • Competence in basic computer applications such as word processing and spreadsheets
  • Excellent verbal and written communication skills for interacting with patients, healthcare professionals, and team members
  • Strong customer service orientation to ensure a compassionate patient experience
  • Knowledge of and adherence to patient privacy regulations and ethical guidelines

Specialized skills

  • Familiarity with medical terminology specific to fertility care
  • Proficiency in promoting the clinic's diverse services and helping to expand the client base
  • Experience using EMR systems specific to fertility clinics
  • Ability to manage appointment scheduling across multiple services, including ultrasounds, lab tests, and consultations
  • Commitment to patient privacy and compliance with applicable standards

Registered Practical Nurse (RPN) / Registered Nurse (RN)

Nursing · Reports to Director of Operations and Nursing Services

The Registered Practical Nurse is responsible for meeting with fertility patients to implement the medication protocol designed by the Medical Director and providing further information/assessments as required. The RPN will work closely with the nursing department and collaborate with all other departments at True Care. This role requires a courteous and compassionate approach to working with patients and providing care.

Responsibilities

  • Meet with fertility patients to implement medication protocols as designed by the Medical Director
  • Act as a liaison to facilitate communication between patients and physicians
  • Arrange for prescription medication ordering and maintain inventory records per College of Physicians and Surgeons of Ontario requirements
  • Schedule procedures and rotate through OR and cycle monitoring positions
  • Maintain patient confidentiality at all times
  • Manage medical supply inventory and place orders as necessary
  • Teach/administer SC and IM injections
  • Provide patient education and support during IVF, FET, and IUI cycles
  • Assist with intrauterine inseminations as directed by physicians
  • Maintain accurate logs of IUI, IVF, and FET procedures
  • Answer patient calls, emails, and messages daily
  • Perform other duties as assigned

Education

  • Current registration with the College of Nurses of Ontario in good standing
  • BCLS certification preferred
  • Diploma or Bachelor's Degree in Life Sciences preferred

Experience

  • 2-3 years of medical/surgical experience, preferably in fertility care
  • Previous work experience in a women's healthcare setting is preferred

Competencies

  • Attention to detail
  • Leadership in innovation and team development
  • Integrity, accountability, and organizational effectiveness
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills
  • Effective verbal and written communication
  • Ability to prioritize, multi-task, and meet schedules
  • Adaptability and poise under stress
  • Team-oriented with a positive attitude

Specialized skills

  • Friendly, courteous, and empathetic patient care
  • Critical thinking and decision-making ability
  • Strong computer and EMR proficiency
  • Ability to work independently and manage patient caseloads
  • Effective teamwork to ensure quality patient care
  • Reliable attendance and performance
  • Preparedness to handle emergency situations
  • Commitment to patient-centered care

Sonographer

Sonography · Reports to Ultrasound Manager

The Sonographer is responsible for performing and evaluating ultrasound examinations for various medical purposes. This role involves assisting doctors in ultrasound-guided procedures, providing accurate diagnoses by reporting and reviewing findings. The Sonographer will operate and maintain ultrasound equipment, actively contribute to team building, and demonstrate the ability to work independently.

Responsibilities

  • Assist doctors in ultrasound-guided procedures (e.g., sonohysterograms)
  • Provide optimum, patient-centered care in a professional and respectful manner
  • Report, review, and record findings from ultrasound scans to support accurate diagnoses
  • Collaborate with physicians regarding ultrasound results
  • Operate and maintain ultrasound equipment; participate in Quality Control and Quality Assurance tests
  • Perform and assess ultrasound examinations (Transabdominal and Transvaginal) for infertility patients, 1st-trimester pregnancy evaluation, endovaginal scan, follicular monitoring, obstetrical scanning (early pregnancy), pelvic ultrasound for gynecology patients
  • Actively participate in team building concepts and development of departmental goals
  • Work independently without supervision
  • Perform other duties as assigned

Education

  • Completion of an accredited degree or post-diploma Ultrasound program (or recognized equivalent)
  • Certification with the College of Medical Radiation and Imaging Technologists of Ontario (CMRITO) in good standing

Experience

  • Minimum 3 years of experience performing and assessing ultrasound examinations
  • Proven proficiency in: transabdominal and transvaginal scanning, sonohysterograms, endovaginal scans, follicular monitoring, early pregnancy obstetric scanning, and gynecologic pelvic ultrasounds

Competencies

  • Sound clinical judgment ensuring patient and team safety
  • High level of technical competency and image quality skills
  • Strong organizational and time management skills
  • Excellent hand-eye coordination
  • Advanced problem-solving and diagnostic recognition abilities

Lab Technician

Technicians · Reports to Director of Operations and Nursing Services

The Lab Technician is responsible for performing blood sample collection, processing samples using the lab machine, ensuring proper patient ID and labeling documentation, determining the appropriate blood drawing method, maintaining accurate records, organizing documents, receiving training in Andrology, maintaining a clean workstation, following infection control standards, and fulfilling assigned duties. Lab technicians are expected to be courteous and sympathetic when working with patients.

Responsibilities

  • Collect blood samples from patients
  • Process samples using a Laboratory machine as dictated by the guidelines
  • Practice proper patient identification and label vials properly
  • Determine the best method for drawing blood depending on the specific patient
  • Keep accurate records on fluid specimens and tests
  • Collect and organize information and documents as required
  • Receive training and perform Andrology tasks (semen analysis, sperm wash, etc.)
  • Keep the phlebotomy cart and station well-organized at all times
  • Practice infection control standards while working with patients and equipment
  • Other duties, as assigned

Education

  • Diploma in Medical Laboratory Technology or equivalent
  • Canadian Society for Medical Laboratory Science (CSMLS) certification is an asset
  • Current registration with the College of Medical Laboratory Technologists of Ontario (CMLTO) in good standing

Experience

  • Minimum of one year of laboratory experience specific to phlebotomy techniques

Competencies

  • Teamwork
  • Attention to detail
  • Leadership including leading innovation, energizing and developing people, delivering outstanding results
  • Integrity and accountability
  • Problem-solving and analytical skills
  • Verbal and written communication skills
  • Organizational skills to prioritize and plan work, multi-task, and meet schedules
  • Adaptable and flexible; able to think quickly in stressful situations

Specialized skills

  • Experience with effective puncture techniques
  • Understanding of proper patient identification methods
  • Knowledge of legal requirements about drawing blood, handling fluid samples, and use of relevant medical equipment and materials
  • Excellent communication and interpersonal skills
  • Ability to work safely and accurately, follow directives from assigned Clinic Manager, and complete assigned tasks promptly
  • Demonstrated ability to work effectively as a team member and adjust to varying work assignments as needed, or independently, as required
  • Experience with a chemistry analyzer is an asset
