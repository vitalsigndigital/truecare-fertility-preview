Privacy policy

How True Care Fertility & Women’s Health handles personal and personal health information.

Template for clinic review. This policy is a plain-language template prepared for True Care Fertility & Women's Health. It must be reviewed and approved by the clinic’s privacy officer (and, where appropriate, legal counsel) before the website is launched.

Who we are

True Care Fertility & Women's Health operates clinics at Suite M1, 414 Victoria Avenue North, Hamilton, Ontario L8L 5G8 and Unit 102, 50 Burnhamthorpe Road West, Mississauga, Ontario L5B 3C2. As a health information custodian in Ontario, the clinic is governed by the Personal Health Information Protection Act, 2004 (PHIPA).

What we collect

We collect the personal health information needed to provide your care — for example, your contact details, health card number, medical history, test results and treatment records — from you, from your referring physician and from laboratories and other providers involved in your care.

How we use and share it

We use and disclose personal health information to provide and support your care, to communicate with your referring physician and other providers in your circle of care, to bill for services, and as permitted or required by law. We do not sell personal information.

Your rights

You may request access to, or correction of, your personal health information, and you may withdraw or limit consent to certain uses and disclosures, subject to legal limits. Contact our privacy officer to make a request. You also have the right to complain to the Information and Privacy Commissioner of Ontario.

This website

  • Contact form. The general enquiry form is for non-medical questions only. It collects your name, contact details and message, which are delivered to reception by a third-party form service. Please do not submit health information through it.
  • Online intake form. The New Patient Intake Form link opens the clinic’s secure patient system; information you submit there becomes part of your health record.
  • Email. Regular email is not secure. Please don’t send health information by email.
  • Analytics. If website analytics are enabled, they record anonymous usage (pages visited, device type) to improve the site. No health information is collected.
  • Maps and video. Location pages embed Google Maps; video visits use Zoom. These services have their own privacy policies.
